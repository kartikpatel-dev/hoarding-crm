# hoarding-crm
This document contains the proposed architecture, feature modules, development roadmap,  technology stack, estimated timeline, and project budget for the B2B Hoarding/Banner  Management CRM system. 

Project Overview 
 Web-based CRM/ERP system for hoarding/banner business management. 
 Frontend: React.js 
 Backend: Laravel API 
 Database: MySQL 
 Languages: English + Gujarati 
 Google Maps pin support 
 PDF invoice/report generation 
 Manual WhatsApp sharing support 

Main Modules 
 Authentication & Role Management 
 Dashboard & Analytics 
 Hoarding/Banner Management 
 Route Management 
 Client & Agency Management 
 Invoice & Billing Management 
 Expense (Kharch) Management 
 Employee & Salary Management 
 Reports & Printing 
 Multi-language support

Important Features 
 Route-wise hoarding filtering 
 Availability status management 
 Multiple photo upload 
 Google Maps pin locations 
 Invoice PDF export 
 Expense tracking 
 Salary management 
 Gujarati + English support 
 Role based permissions
